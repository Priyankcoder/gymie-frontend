## 🎨 Generation Strategy

### Step 1: Generate All Options
Use your preferred AI tool (DALL-E 3, Midjourney, Leonardo AI) to generate all 10 concepts.

### Step 2: Create Comparison Grid
Save all generated icons with names:
- `option1-strength-progress.png`
- `option2-power-badge.png`
- `option3-minimal-dumbbell.png`
- etc.

### Step 3: Evaluate
For each icon, check:
- ✅ Recognizable at 20x20px?
- ✅ Works on light background?
- ✅ Works on dark background?
- ✅ Professional appearance?
- ✅ Unique and memorable?
- ✅ Matches app purpose?
- ✅ No text/clear at small sizes?

### Step 4: Get Feedback
- Show to 3-5 people
- Ask: "Which feels most professional?"
- Ask: "Which would you trust?"
- Ask: "Which makes you want to use the app?"

### Step 5: Refine Winner
Once you pick a favorite:
- Generate 2-3 color variations
- Try slight modifications
- Test with your brand colors
- Finalize and export

## 💡 Pro Tips

**Color Variations to Try:**
- Blue → Purple gradient (default)
- Blue → Cyan gradient (cooler)
- Purple → Pink gradient (bolder)
- Solid blue (simpler)
- Dark mode version (light icon on dark)

**Modifications:**
- Rounded corners vs sharp
- 2D flat vs 2.5D depth
- Thick lines vs thin lines
- Icon size (bigger/smaller in frame)

**Testing:**
```bash
# After selecting your favorite:
1. Save as: frontend/assets/images/icon.png (1024x1024)
2. Generate all sizes: npx expo prebuild --clean
3. Test in app: npm run android
```
