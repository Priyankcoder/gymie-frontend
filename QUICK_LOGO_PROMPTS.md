## 🏆 RECOMMENDED: Universal Prompt

**Use this with DALL-E 3, Midjourney, Leonardo AI, or Stable Diffusion:**

```
Create a modern minimalist mobile app icon for "Gymie" fitness tracking app. Design features a stylized geometric dumbbell merging with an upward trending arrow or growth line, symbolizing strength and progress. Background is a smooth gradient from electric blue (#2196F3) to deep purple (#9C27B0). The dumbbell icon should be white or light cyan, clean and simple. Style is flat 2.5D with subtle depth, professional yet energetic. 1024x1024px square format. High contrast, works at small sizes. No text or typography. Empowering, tech-forward aesthetic that hints at AI-powered tracking.
```

## 🎨 Design Specs

```
Size: 1024x1024px
Colors: #2196F3 (blue) → #9C27B0 (purple)
Icon: White (#FFFFFF) or Light Cyan (#E6F4FE)
Style: Flat 2.5D, minimalist
Format: PNG with transparency
```

---

## ✅ Quick Check

Your generated icon should:
- ✅ Be recognizable at 20x20px
- ✅ Work on light AND dark backgrounds
- ✅ Have no text/typography
- ✅ Be simple and geometric
- ✅ Feel professional yet energetic

## 📦 Next Steps

1. **Generate**: Copy prompt → Paste into AI tool
2. **Download**: Save as `icon.png` (1024x1024px)
3. **Place**: Move to `frontend/assets/images/icon.png`
4. **Generate sizes**: Use online tool or:
   ```bash
   npx expo prebuild --clean
   ```
